# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Homeowners in Ranchi who need dependable home-appliance repair. They are often dealing with a disrupted household routine and need to quickly confirm that a qualified local professional can help.

## Product Purpose

Hindustan Care connects Ranchi homeowners with appliance-repair experts for same-day, dependable doorstep service. Success is measured by more qualified phone enquiries, with WhatsApp available for visitors who prefer to message first.

## Positioning

Ranchi's trusted appliance repair experts delivering same-day service, transparent pricing, and repairs you can rely on with after-service support.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary and secondary CTA: Call now; WhatsApp enquiry.
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: Same-day appliance repairs in Ranchi, with transparent pricing and support after the repair.
- Belief ladder: Hindustan Care can repair my appliance; they can arrive promptly; their pricing will be fair and clear; the repair will be dependable and supported afterwards.
- Proof on hand: Customer testimonials and ratings.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, premium, and modern, with professional clarity and a sense of speed. The experience should make visitors feel the service is genuine and worth calling right away. Its values are reliability, transparency, local expertise, and capable service.

## Anti-references

Avoid cheap local-repair directories, generic WordPress templates, outdated government-style sites, and overly flashy startup landing pages. Keep the experience clean and conversion-focused without sacrificing clarity.

## Design Principles

1. Make trust concrete through clear service promises, testimonials, ratings, and practical after-service support.
2. Make the next action immediate: calling is always obvious, with WhatsApp as a low-friction alternative.
3. Signal expert local service through precise, professional language rather than bargain-led messaging.
4. Keep decisions easy under household urgency: concise information, legible hierarchy, and transparent expectations.
5. Deliver premium restraint: modern polish without startup spectacle or generic template conventions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Use an accessible baseline: clear language, strong color contrast, keyboard-operable controls, descriptive alternative text, and reduced-motion support. No additional requirements have been specified.
